## Participating national statistical offices

The training programme that produced this material ran from May to August 2026
with teams from eight offices, whose questions, data problems and project work
shaped what the toolkit covers and how much detail each step needed:

- Bhutan — National Statistics Bureau
- Dominican Republic — Oficina Nacional de Estadística
- Jamaica — Statistical Institute of Jamaica
- Kenya — Kenya National Bureau of Statistics
- Namibia — Namibia Statistics Agency
- Rwanda — National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda
- Somalia — National Bureau of Statistics
- State of Palestine — Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics

## Data

The synthetic registers used throughout the toolkit reproduce the frequency and
error structure of administrative registers held by the **Instituto Nacional de
Estadísticas (INE) of Chile**, which supplied the frequency tables they were
generated from. They contain no real records. See
[`data/README.md`](../../data/README.md).

## Contributing

This is a living resource, and it is open beyond the eight offices above.

**Country practices.** If your office has implemented record linkage and is
using it in the production of official statistics, we would like to publish it.
The criterion is implementation: a case is included once linkage is in
production or in use, not while it is still a proposal. The submission outline is in Section 5, Country case examples.

**Corrections and questions.** Open an issue in the
[repository](https://github.com/NicolasLibuy/record-linkage-toolkit/issues).
Recurring questions end up in the FAQ, which is maintained as a running list
rather than published once and left alone.
